## 🌟 **Welcome to the Future of Listening**

NevPlay is not just a music player—it is an **Intelligence Lab** for your ears. Designed with a meticulous "Fluent" aesthetic and powered by next-generation AI, NevPlay is engineered to redefine the mobile audio experience. Whether you are navigating a massive local library or streaming from your personal server, NevPlay delivers it with unparalleled beauty and precision.

---

## 🚀 **The NevPlay Marquee Features**

### 🧠 **The Intelligence Lab (AI Core)**
Unlock features previously reserved for expensive cloud services, handled locally or via your preferred LLM:
- **Real-time Lyrics Translation**: Seamlessly translate foreign tracks into your native language.
- **AI Playlist Architect**: Generate mood-perfect playlists from a simple text prompt.
- **Deep Song Insights**: Discover the story, mood, and technical details behind every beat.

### 🎨 **Fluent & Expressive Design**
- **Material You 3.0**: A UI that breathes with your wallpaper colors.
- **Expressive Shapes**: Organic, adaptive forms that make every interaction feel alive.
- **Multi-Pane Tablet UI**: A desktop-grade experience for large-screen devices.

### 🔊 **Pro-Grade Audio Engine**
- **Gapless High-Res Playback**: Powered by Media3 ExoPlayer & custom FFmpeg decoders.
- **AutoEQ Integration**: Instant access to 6000+ professional headphone profiles.
- **Dual-Mode Sync**: Fluidly switch between Local Offline and NevPlay GO (Streaming) modes.

## 🛠️ **Technology Stack**
NevPlay is built on the bleeding edge of Android development:
- **Core:** 100% Kotlin with Clean Architecture + MVVM.
- **UI:** Jetpack Compose, Material 3, and Glance.
- **Audio:** Media3 ExoPlayer, JAudioTagger, and custom FFmpeg build.
- **Database:** Room Persistence with SQLite.
