NevPlay Music Player

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 4, 2026

"Your music, your data, your privacy. NevPlay is engineered for those who demand absolute data sovereignty."

1. Privacy by Design

NevPlay is a professional-grade music experience. We operate on a strict zero-knowledge policy. We do not harvest, track, or sell your personal information. Your digital footprint remains entirely within your control.

2. Data Ownership

We do not collect personally identifiable information. We believe that your listening habits are private; therefore, we do not monitor your behavior, location, or metadata. You are the sole owner of the data stored on your device.

3. Local-First Architecture

All app configurations, playlists, and playback statistics are stored exclusively in your device's secure, local environment. We do not maintain any cloud servers capable of syncing or viewing your local library.

4. Network & Permissions

NevPlay requests only the minimum permissions necessary for core operation. Network usage occurs exclusively when you invoke specific features, such as fetching lyrics. Even then, we only transmit the minimal necessary query metadata. No tracking identifiers are ever attached to these requests.

5. Integrity & Professionalism

Our commitment to your privacy is absolute. By keeping our architecture simple and localized, we eliminate the risks inherent in data-harvesting business models.
