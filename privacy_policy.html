Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 4, 2026

Our Commitment: NevPlay is built on a "Local-First" philosophy. We respect your digital sovereignty; therefore, we do not track, harvest, or monetize your personal data.

1. The Core Philosophy

NevPlay is a professional-grade music player engineered for transparency. Your data remains strictly on your device. We operate on a zero-knowledge basis regarding your personal information, listening habits, or library content.

2. Data Sovereignty

We do not collect any personally identifiable information. This includes, but is not limited to: your name, email, location, device advertising IDs, or usage behavioral metrics. When you use NevPlay, you are the sole owner of your data.

3. Local Storage Architecture

Any configuration, playlist metadata, or playback statistics generated are stored exclusively within the secure, local sandbox of your Android device. We possess no cloud infrastructure to synchronize or view this information.

4. Feature-Specific Network Usage

NevPlay may initiate network requests only when explicitly invoked by you to enhance your media library, such as fetching lyrics or metadata. These requests contain only the absolute minimum required metadata (e.g., track title, artist). No user-identifiable tokens or persistent tracking identifiers are transmitted.

5. Permission Transparency

We only request Android system permissions necessary for core playback functionality (e.g., media file access, notification display). We audit these requirements strictly to ensure they are the minimum privilege needed for the specific task at hand.

6. Third-Party Interactions

Where functionality involves third-party APIs, we prioritize providers who demonstrate a commitment to privacy. When using these features, you interact with their services directly according to their respective policies. We do not act as an intermediary for your data.

7. Commitment to Integrity

NevPlay is engineered for excellence and security. We maintain rigorous standards for code quality and user trust.
